Got Questions?
Frequently Asked Questions
🎟️ Registration & Passes
Who can attend Eminence 2026?
Eminence 2026 is open to all students from colleges and universities across India. VIT Vellore students get free entry with their student ID. Outstation students and guests can purchase passes at the gate or register online for discounted rates.
How do I register for events and competitions?
Head to the Events page, browse the event you wish to enter, click "Register", select an available slot, and complete payment through our secure checkout. You will receive a confirmation email with your registration ID — bring this to the venue on the day.
Can I participate in multiple events?
Absolutely! There is no limit on the number of events you can register for, as long as timings do not clash. Check the Schedule page carefully before registering for multiple events on the same day.
Is the Ascend Night pass included in the general fest pass?
No. The Ascend Night 10th Anniversary Concert requires a separate ticket priced at ₹299 per person. Tickets are limited to 2,000 and expected to sell out quickly — register early on the Special Event page.
What is the refund policy?
Registrations are non-refundable once confirmed. However, if an event is cancelled by the organizers, full refunds will be processed within 7 working days to your original payment method. For team events, the registration can be transferred to another team member free of charge up to 48 hours before the event.
💳 Payments & Checkout
What payment methods are accepted?
We accept all major UPI apps (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), debit and credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), and net banking. All payments are processed through our secure checkout with 256-bit SSL encryption.
Is online payment mandatory for event registration?
Paid events require online payment at the time of registration to confirm your slot. Free events can be registered for without any payment. Walk-in registrations may be available at the venue subject to availability, but online registration is strongly recommended to guarantee your spot.
Will I receive a receipt after payment?
Yes! A confirmation email with your unique registration ID and payment receipt will be sent to your email address within minutes of completing checkout. Save this — you'll need it at the venue. If you don't receive it within 30 minutes, check your spam folder or contact us at eminence@vit.ac.in.
Can I apply a coupon or discount code at checkout?
Yes, the checkout page has a coupon field. Official discount codes are announced on our Instagram and Twitter pages. VIT students may be eligible for special discount codes — check your college email for an exclusive offer.
🛍️ Merchandise
How do I order official Eminence merchandise?
Visit the Merch Store, click "Add to Bag" on any item, and complete checkout. Pre-ordered merchandise gets a 10% early bird discount and fast-track pickup at the venue stall.
Where can I pick up my pre-ordered merchandise?
Pre-ordered merchandise can be picked up at the Eminence Merch Stall at the Main Campus Gate from April 27–30, 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM daily. Bring your order confirmation ID for fast-track collection. Walk-in purchases are available subject to stock.
🏨 Accommodation & Travel
Is there accommodation available for outstation participants?
Yes! We have limited hostel accommodation for outstation participants on a first-come, first-served basis. Accommodation packages (3 nights, April 27–30) are available for ₹999/person. Email eminence@vit.ac.in with the subject "Accommodation Request" to book.
How do I get to VIT Vellore campus?
VIT Vellore is located at Katpadi, Vellore, Tamil Nadu — 632014. The nearest railway station is Katpadi Junction (3 km away) and the nearest airport is Chennai International (~140 km). Eminence shuttle buses will run between Katpadi station and campus every 30 minutes during fest days, 7 AM – 11 PM.
Is parking available on campus?
Free parking is available in the visitor parking area near the Main Gate. Two-wheelers can park adjacent to the Academic Block. Due to high footfall we recommend using public transport or the Eminence shuttle from Katpadi station.
📋 General
What are the fest timings?
Eminence 2026 runs from April 18–20. Gates open at 8:00 AM each day. Most events begin at 9:00 AM. The Ascend Night Concert on April 20 runs until 11:00 PM. See the Schedule page for a full day-by-day breakdown.
What should I bring to the fest?
Bring your college ID, all registration confirmation IDs (screenshot or printout), a valid photo ID (Aadhaar/passport), and cash for food stalls and on-site merch. For performances or technical events, check the specific event rules on the Events page for any equipment you may need.
Is food available at the venue?
Yes! VIT food courts and canteens will be open throughout. There will also be special food stalls by popular vendors — street food, desserts, and regional cuisine — set up in the Fest Zone near the open-air theatre. Swiggy is our food delivery partner with a dedicated drop zone on campus.
Are there any rules or code of conduct?
All participants must follow VIT Vellore's campus rules and the Eminence Code of Conduct. Alcohol, drugs, and prohibited items are strictly not allowed on campus. Disruptive behaviour may result in disqualification from events and removal from the premises. The organizing team's decisions are final in all matters.
